## Project Context
This is the internal workspace for **AquaTerra** (@ngo.aquaterra), a youth-led organization in Kolkata, India. Not a traditional NGO — it's a hybrid ecosystem combining environmental activism, student startups, a streetwear brand (ROOTS), an edtech platform (ShikshAQ), and a free marketing agency (AQ.Ventures).

## Content Generation Rules
When creating content for AquaTerra, ALWAYS:
1. Check `aq-master-brief.md` Section 5 for **banned phrases** before outputting
2. Apply the **Real Person Test** (Section 4, Rule 1) — include real names/numbers/anecdotes
3. Use the correct **voice**: informal, Hinglish, lowercase, short sentences, self-deprecating humor
4. Keep philosophy under 20%, proof over 80%
5. For reels: ZERO AQ mentions in the video itself
6. For CTAs: soft, open-door energy only ("link in bio if you want in" is the ceiling)
7. Run the **Section 21 checklist** before finalizing any content

## Brand Voice Summary
Sound like "your most interesting friend telling you about the insane thing their team just pulled off." Never sound like an institution, a school program, or a corporate internship ad. Hinglish is native. Chaos is intentional.
