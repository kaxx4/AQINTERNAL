## 🔒 Brand Voice Standards (Agent-Enforced)

All agents MUST follow these AquaTerra rules:

### ✅ Mandatory
- First person plural ("we", not "AquaTerra")
- Lowercase default (casual tone)
- Hinglish fluency (natural, not performed)
- Real person test: every carousel needs names/numbers/anecdotes
- Proof-over-philosophy: 80% proof minimum
- Soft CTAs: "link in bio if you want in" (never "JOIN NOW!!")

### ❌ Banned Phrases
- "A student-run NGO where teenagers..."
- "Your schedule doesn't define your impact"
- "Teenagers with ideas have always been..."
- "No resume/experience required" (standalone)
- "Join AQ NOW!!" or any variation with !!

### 🎭 Tone Dimensions
- **Formality**: 15/100 (sentence fragments, casual)
- **Humor**: 75/100 (self-deprecating, observational absurdism)
- **Chaos**: 65/100 (unhinged but intentional)
- **Inspiration**: 40/100 (emerge from story, never lead with it)
- **Vulnerability**: 55/100 (okay to say "we had no idea", not a therapy session)
