# The Opportunities at AquaTerra

*A student community where real work happens.*

AQ started with welfare projects.
But over time something unexpected happened.

Students didn't just run drives.

They started designing brands.
Running events.
Publishing magazines.
Talking to CEOs.
Building startups.
Launching products.

What began as projects slowly became something bigger — **a community where students gain exposure far beyond school.**

Here's what that actually looks like.

---

# 1. Creative & Media

*For people who like building things the internet sees.*

### Social Media Strategy

Help decide what thousands of people see when they open Instagram. You'll plan content, track trends, and turn chaotic student ideas into posts that actually perform.

### Reel Creation & Editing

Shoot, edit, and publish reels that tell the story of AQ. From event clips to viral concepts, you're the reason the internet knows we exist.

### Graphic Design

Design posts, carousels, posters, and visual campaigns. If you like turning ideas into visuals people instantly understand, this is your playground.

### Illustration & Visual Art

Create artwork for campaigns, publications, and storytelling projects. Your drawings could become part of posters, magazines, or even merch.

### Content Writing

Write captions, stories, articles, and creative pieces about what AQ does. It's storytelling, but about real things students are actually building.

### Photography & Visual Documentation

Capture drives, events, and projects as they happen. You'll be the one turning chaotic moments into images that tell the story later.

### Video Production

Plan, shoot, and produce longer-form videos — documentaries, interviews, and project coverage.

---

# 2. Technology & Digital Building

*For people who want to actually build things on the internet.*

### Website Development

Work on AQ's websites — designing the front end, fixing the backend, and experimenting with AI tools to improve the platform.

### Platform Building

Help build and maintain digital systems that run parts of the AQ ecosystem.

### Digital Product Experimentation

Test new tools, systems, and platforms that help the community operate more efficiently.

### Data & Systems Management

Maintain databases, lists, and digital infrastructure that keep projects organized.

---

# 3. Outreach & Partnerships

*For people who want to talk to the world outside AQ.*

### NGO & Organization Partnerships

Reach out to NGOs, companies, and communities to build collaborations. It's real-world networking before you even graduate school.

### School Outreach

Coordinate with schools to host workshops, tests, and collaborative initiatives.

### Influencer & Creator Collaborations

Work with creators and public figures who can amplify campaigns and projects.

### CSR & Corporate Conversations

Present ideas to professionals and decision-makers who could support or collaborate with student initiatives.

---

# 4. Community & People Leadership

*For people who like organizing people and making things run smoothly.*

### HR & Community Management

Help onboard new members, manage teams, and make sure the community stays organized and functional.

### Team Coordination

Support multiple teams and help projects move from idea to execution.

### Internal Communication

Keep the massive AQ community aligned, informed, and connected.

### Member Engagement

Design ways for students to meet each other, collaborate, and feel part of something bigger than just projects.

---

# 5. Event Creation

*For people who want to build experiences from scratch.*

### Event Core Team

Plan and run large AQ events from concept to execution.

### Event Marketing

Promote events, design promotional campaigns, and make sure people actually show up.

### Sponsorship & Partnerships

Convince brands and organizations to support events financially or strategically.

### Decor & Experience Design

Design how an event looks and feels — stages, spaces, themes, and atmosphere.

### Production & Technical Setup

Handle sound, lighting, music, DJs, and technical elements that make events work.

### Sports Event Organization

Coordinate sports competitions, tournaments, and activity-based events.

---

# 6. Social Impact & Welfare Projects

*For people who want to work on real ground-level initiatives.*

### Campaign Creation

Design and run campaigns like dog feeding drives, clothing collection programs, and donation initiatives.

### Welfare Drives

Coordinate distribution drives, relief work, and community support activities.

### Workshop Facilitation

Conduct workshops for students and communities on topics like sustainability, awareness, and life skills.

### Educational Resource Creation

Build worksheets, activities, and learning materials for workshops.

### Project Logistics

Manage resources, volunteers, and operations during campaigns and drives.

---

# 7. Publishing & Storytelling

*For people who want to build something that lasts.*

### Magazine Writing

Write articles, essays, or creative pieces for AQ's digital and print publications.

### Editorial & Publishing

Edit, curate, and shape the content that becomes part of the magazine.

### Magazine Design

Design layouts and visual storytelling for a professional-looking publication.

### Creative Writing

Contribute poetry, stories, or experimental writing pieces.

### Illustration for Publication

Create artwork that accompanies written stories and articles.

---

# 8. Startup Experience (ShikshAQ)

*A real student-run startup environment.*

### Teacher Outreach

Connect with teachers and help bring them onto the ShikshAQ platform.

### Customer Support & User Coordination

Help users find the right tutors and ensure the platform runs smoothly for students and parents.

### Startup Marketing

Promote the platform and help it grow its user base.

### Platform Operations

Maintain the systems that allow ShikshAQ to function daily.

### Product Development

Experiment with ideas that make the platform more useful.

---

# 9. Fashion & Product Building (ROOTS)

*Running a streetwear brand from the inside.*

### Fashion Design

Conceptualize and design clothing pieces for ROOTS collections.

### Graphic Design for Apparel

Create prints, artwork, and visuals that appear on garments.

### Manufacturing & Sourcing

Coordinate with suppliers and manufacturers to turn designs into real products.

### Order Logistics

Manage packaging, delivery, and fulfillment of orders.

### B2B & Bulk Orders

Handle larger partnerships where institutions order merchandise.

### Brand Social Media

Tell the story of ROOTS online and build its audience.

### School Partnerships

Work with schools and communities to bring ROOTS to new audiences.

---

# 10. Real Student Community Experiences

*Things that don't fit into departments but happen anyway.*

### Cross-School Collaboration

Meet and work with students from completely different schools and backgrounds.

### Project Experimentation

Pitch random ideas and sometimes turn them into real projects.

### Skill Discovery

Discover abilities you didn't know you had because someone asked you to try something.

### Peer Learning

Learn things from other students that school never teaches.

### Community Building

Make friendships and connections with people who care about building things.

---

**In simple terms:**

You might join AQ thinking you'll volunteer on a few projects.

And six months later you might find yourself:

* designing a campaign
* organizing an event
* building a website
* writing for a magazine
* managing a team
* or launching a product

That's the weird thing about AQ.

You don't just join projects.

You **accidentally gain experience doing real things.**
